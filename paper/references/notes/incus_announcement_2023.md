# Incus Announcement

**Author:** Sarai, Aleksa
**Year:** 2023
**Source:** Linux Containers official website
**URL:** https://linuxcontainers.org/incus/announcement/

## Summary

Official announcement of the Incus project by Aleksa Sarai on behalf of the Linux Containers community. Formalizes the fork of LXD after Canonical withdrew it from the community project in July 2023. Establishes Incus under the Linux Containers umbrella with Apache 2.0 license and no CLA requirement.

## Key Findings

- Fork was initiated by the original LXD community developers
- Adopted under Linux Containers organization (same as LXC, LXCFS)
- Apache 2.0 license, no Contributor License Agreement
- Goal: community-led development without corporate gatekeeping
- First stable release: Incus 0.1 (October 2023)
