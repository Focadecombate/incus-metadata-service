# Incus as an alternative to LXD

**Author:** Stéphane Gräber
**Year:** 2023
**Source:** Personal blog (stgraber.org)
**URL:** https://stgraber.org/2023/08/08/introducing-incus/

## Summary

Blog post by Stéphane Gräber, the original creator and lead developer of LXD, announcing the Incus project. Explains the motivation for the fork: Canonical's decision to end community participation in LXD development and move it in-house. Gräber details his vision for Incus as a fully community-led alternative, maintaining the LXD architecture and feature set while being developed openly under the Linux Containers umbrella.

## Key Findings

- LXD was created by Gräber at Canonical and was a Linux Containers community project
- In July 2023, Canonical withdrew LXD from the community project
- Gräber, no longer at Canonical, initiated the fork with support from the original LXD team
- Aleksa Sarai formally announced Incus under Linux Containers in August 2023
- First stable release: Incus 0.1 in October 2023
- Key people: Gräber, Sarai, Christian Brauner, Serge Hallyn, Tycho Andersen
